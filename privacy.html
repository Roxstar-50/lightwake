LIGHTWAKE

Privacy policy. Effective November 4, 2026.

Lightwake is a light bike racing game. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Lightwake has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send any information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in your device's own storage:

  • Your settings, such as ribbon colour, difficulty, laps and whether sound is on.
  • Your best lap times, top scores and time trial ghost laps for each track.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Links to other sites

The main menu has a link to the developer's X profile. Opening it takes you to X, and X's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

Lightwake does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
